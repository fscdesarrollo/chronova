# Chronova — Brand

**Product name:** Chronova  
**Tagline:** Adaptive Planning Timeline  
**Slogan:** *See time. Shape what comes next.*

---

## Name meaning

**Chronova** combines:

- **Chrono** — time, sequence, the history of the plan
- **Nova** — renewal, a new reading of the future

The name reflects a core idea: you cannot stop time or rewrite the past, but you can make it **visible** and adapt what comes next.

## Philosophy

In real planning, almost everything eventually shows up in time:

- teams with different performance levels
- vague or incomplete requirements
- early or late deliveries
- unexpected circumstances that shift the plan

**Time keeps moving no matter what.** What you can do is:

1. **See** how work evolves over time
2. **Preserve** the original plan (baseline) without denying it
3. **Detect** deviations early
4. **Act** before a delay becomes a bigger problem
5. **Learn** from the past to improve tomorrow — without trying to change that past

## What Chronova does

Chronova is an **adaptive planning timeline**: a simple PI view for teams and stakeholders, focused on executive clarity and early action — not a replacement for Jira or Azure DevOps.

| Principle | In the app |
|-----------|------------|
| The past stays recorded | Baseline + audit log |
| The present is visible | Current view, Gantt bars, deviations |
| The future can be corrected | Drag-and-drop, reassignment, early warnings |
| Clarity is the goal | Project/team/feature view in seconds |

## Elevator pitch

> Chronova does not try to stop time — it makes time visible so you can adapt the plan and reach your goals.
